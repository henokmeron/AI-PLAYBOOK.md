# Fictional Company Handbook

## Office hours
The support office is staffed Monday to Friday from 09:00 to 17:30 UK time. The office is closed on UK public holidays unless a manager has announced an exception.

## Working arrangements
Employees should agree their normal working arrangements with their line manager. Requests for changes to working patterns should be sent to the line manager before the change begins.

## Information handling
Employees must store company documents only in approved company systems. Sensitive customer information must not be copied to personal email or personal cloud storage. Report suspected loss or accidental disclosure of company information to the security contact promptly.
