# Fictional Company Expenses Policy

## Receipts and submission
Employees must submit expense claims with receipts within 10 business days of the expense. If a receipt is missing, the employee must explain why and ask their manager how to proceed.

## Approval
Any single expense above £100 requires manager approval before purchase, except for a documented emergency. Splitting one purchase into multiple claims to avoid the approval threshold is not permitted.

## Travel
Employees should book rail travel in standard class and book in advance where reasonably possible. A manager must approve an exception before first-class rail travel is booked.

## Reimbursement
Approved, complete expense claims are normally reimbursed in the next monthly payment run. This is a target timescale, not a guarantee if information is missing or the claim needs clarification.
