## What this proves

- The script can load Markdown files from the selected folder.
- Keyword/BM25 retrieval placed the relevant expense policy first for this question.

## What it does not prove

- It does not prove semantic retrieval quality, broad factual reliability, or that citations are always accurate.
- It does not test unsupported questions, malicious instructions embedded in documents, access control, tenant separation, PDF/Office parsing, or production security.
- The implementation is a prototype and has no authentication, document-level permissions, audit logging, or production deployment guardrails.
